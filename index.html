Services & Solutions

Our main core services & solutions

1. Fleet Strategy & Optimization Studies
We analyze fleet composition, utilization, and cost drivers to help clients develop efficient and sustainable fleet strategies.
2. Maintenance Systems Design & Implementation
We establish modern workshop systems, job card processes, preventive maintenance schedules, and inventory controls.
3. Fuel Management Services
We help organizations gain control over their fuel usage through accurate monitoring, auditing, and automation.
Tyre Management Services
Provide complete tyre management solutions that enhance safety, reduce operating costs, and extend tyre life.
Fleet Financial Management
Developing cost control frameworks, lifecycle costing, budgeting tools, and financial models that help organizations plan and manage operational expenses effectively.
4. Transport Policy & SOP Development
We draft and implement tailored transport policies, operational standards, and compliance manuals that align with safety, sustainability, and corporate goals.

i. Fleet and Workshop Establishment
Full development of fleet workshops including management systems, equipment and tooling and staffing.
ii. Fuel Management Services
Installation of automated fuel level sensors, system integration with telematics, and development of real-time fuel monitoring dashboards to reduce losses and improve efficiency.
iii. Tyre Management Services
We also design and implement tyre tracking systems, tyre store layouts, and data integration tools that ensure full visibility of tyre usage and costs across the fleet.
iv. Transport System Digitization (ERP, SAP, or TMS Integration)
We help organizations implement digital fleet and logistics management systems such as SAP S/4HANA, TMS, and Fleet maintenance Software modules to achieve visibility and automation.
v. Industrial Facilities Development & Upgrade
Roads, water systems and buildings
vi. Renewable Energy & Green Transport Installations
We deliver solar-powered workshop lighting, EV charging points, and energy-efficient systems to support sustainability initiatives.

Fleet Associates Africa offers professional outsourcing for drivers, mechanics, technicians, and industrial management staff. We handle recruitment, training, and performance management for organizations seeking operational flexibility and quality assurance.
Benefits include:
  • Access to vetted and skilled professionals
  • Reduced HR and administrative costs
  • Improved operational uptime and compliance
  • Flexible staffing models (short-term or long-term)

We design and deliver targeted training programs to strengthen workforce skills in fleet, workshop, and industrial operations. Our goal is to build competent, safety-conscious, and data-driven teams that enhance asset performance and cost efficiency.
Our areas of Focus include:
i. Driver Training Programs
Defensive driver training, Eco driver training, special driver packages for Articulated driver, tipper driver and plant operators (Forklifts, construction machinery and cranes).
ii. Management & Technical Training
Fleet supervisory and Management trainings, Industrial managers trainings and technical training for maintenance teams.
