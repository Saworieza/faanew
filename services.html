Specialized Expertise

Fleet Projects, Consulting & Training Solutions

We provide specialized services that help organizations design, optimize, and manage vehicle fleets in complex operational environments.

Our solutions combine engineering expertise, operational experience, digital systems, and professional training to improve fleet reliability, efficiency, safety, and sustainability.

We support organizations across transport, logistics, conservation, government, development programs, and commercial fleets throughout Africa.

1. Project Management

Fleet Associates provides end-to-end implementation of fleet & industrial projects, especially for organizations.

Fleet and Workshop Establishment

Full development of fleet workshops including management systems, equipment and tooling and staffing.

Fuel Management Services

Installation of automated fuel level sensors, system integration with telematics, and real-time fuel monitoring dashboards to reduce losses.

Tyre Management Services

Design and implementation of tyre tracking systems, tyre store layouts, and data integration tools that ensure full visibility of tyre usage and costs.

Transport System Digitization

Implementation of digital fleet and logistics management systems such as SAP S/4HANA, TMS, and Fleet maintenance Software modules.

Industrial Facilities Development & Upgrade

Professional construction, maintenance, and upgrading of essential infrastructure including roads, water systems and buildings.

Renewable Energy & Green Transport

Delivery of solar-powered workshop lighting, EV charging points, and energy-efficient systems to support sustainability initiatives.

2. Consultancy Based Solutions

Fleet Strategy & Optimization Studies

Maintenance Systems Design & Implementation

Fuel Management Services

Tyre Management Services
