Specialized Expertise

Fleet Engineering, Consulting & Training Solutions

We provide specialized services that help organizations design, optimize, and manage vehicle fleets in complex operational environments.

Our solutions combine engineering expertise, operational experience, digital systems, and professional training to improve fleet reliability, efficiency, safety, and sustainability. We support transport, logistics, conservation, and government programs throughout Africa.

What We Do

Three Specialized Verticals

1. Fleet Projects

Implementation of Complete Fleet Systems in remote or challenging environments.

  • Motor pool establishment
  • Workshop setup & optimization
  • Fleet Digitalization (Telematics/FMS)
  • Green Fleet (EV/Hybrid) Integration
  • Asset replacement strategies

2. Fleet Consultancy

Strategic advisory to improve operational efficiency and strengthen governance.

  • Fleet Operational Audits
  • Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
  • Fuel & Tyre Management Systems
  • Procurement Strategy & Tenders
  • Road Safety Audits

3. Training Solutions

Professional technical training combining practical knowledge with modern technology.

  • Fleet Management Training
  • Automotive Diagnostics Training
  • Defensive & Eco-Driving
  • Electric Mobility Maintenance
  • 5S Workshop Organization
